=== ETHNODOCK TCM PHARMACOPEIA RECORD ===
Species Common Name: Magnolia Bark
Scientific Taxon: Magnolia officinalis
Traditional Chinese Name: 厚朴
Category: Traditional Chinese Botanical Medicine

--- TRADITIONAL PROPERTIES & MERIDIAN TROPISM ---
Nature (Four Natures): Slightly Warm / Neutral
Taste (Five Flavors): Sweet, Slightly Bitter
Channel Tropism: Spleen, Lung, Heart Meridians

--- TRADITIONAL FUNCTIONS & INDICATIONS ---
1. Tonifies Spleen Qi and augments vital organ energy reserves.
2. Strengthens defensive Qi (Wei Qi) and stabilizes the exterior matrix.
3. Promotes fluid circulation and relieves interior edema.
4. Classical Pharmacopeia Citation: Shennong Bencao Jing (Superior Grade Herb).

--- CLASSICAL FORMULA COMPOSITIONS ---
- Formula 1: Sijunzi Tang (Four Gentlemen Decoction) - Role: Chief (Jun)
- Formula 2: Bu Zhong Yi Qi Tang (Tonify the Middle Decoction) - Role: Deputy (Chen)
- Formula 3: Yu Ping Feng San (Jade Windscreen Powder) - Role: Assistant (Zuo)
