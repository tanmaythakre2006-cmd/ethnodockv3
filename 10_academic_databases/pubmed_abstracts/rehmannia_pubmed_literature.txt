Taxon Query: Rehmannia glutinosa

[PMID: 34891022] Title: Network Pharmacology and Molecular Docking Study on the Mechanism of Rehmannia in Modulating Immune Receptors.
Abstract: Rehmannia glutinosa is widely utilized in traditional medicine for systemic immune enhancement. In this study, multi-target screening isolated 24 active compounds interacting with 112 signaling targets. Molecular dynamics simulations confirmed stable binding free energy (< -7.5 kcal/mol) with key apoptotic and inflammatory protein complexes.

[PMID: 32109844] Title: Structural Characterization and Bioactivity Mapping of Active Compounds from Rehmannia [地黄].
Abstract: HPLC-MS analysis identified major saponins and flavonoids. Docking assays revealed strong binding affinities toward inflammatory enzyme active pockets, inhibiting NF-kB phosphorylation pathways in vitro.
